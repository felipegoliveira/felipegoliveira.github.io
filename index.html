About

About Me

I am a professor and researcher at the Federal University of Amazonas (UFAM), where I have been based at the Institute of Exact Sciences and Technology (ICET) in Itacoatiara since 2012. I teach in the Software Engineering and Information Systems programs and am also a permanent faculty member of UFAM’s Graduate Program in Informatics (PPGI), working in the Computer Vision and Mobile Robotics research line.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and an M.Sc. in Informatics from UFAM, and a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), including a research period at CSIRO in Australia. I later completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG).

My research lies at the intersection of Mobile Robotics, Computer Vision, and Artificial Intelligence. I am particularly interested in robotic perception, image processing and enhancement, multimodal sensing, autonomous navigation, path planning, and deep learning, with applications ranging from environmental monitoring and industrial inspection to digital content authentication.

I coordinate and collaborate on research and development projects supported by national funding agencies and industry partners, working with institutions in Brazil and abroad. Current initiatives include robotic systems for environmental monitoring in the Amazon and DeepSafe, a project developed in partnership with Motorola for video authentication on mobile devices.

Beyond research, I am actively involved in graduate supervision, scientific collaboration, and the organization of robotics-related academic events. I am also a founder and currently a council member of the Brazilian Robotics Society (SBRobotica).
