About

About Me

I am a professor and researcher working in the areas of Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Robotics. My research focuses on the development of intelligent perception and autonomous systems capable of operating in complex and challenging environments.

My current research interests include mobile robotics, visual perception, autonomous navigation, multimodal sensing, deep learning, environmental monitoring, and intelligent systems for aquatic and Amazonian environments.

I am also involved in research projects and academic collaborations focused on applying artificial intelligence and robotics to scientific, technological, environmental, and societal challenges.
